### Workspace

- Pinned tabs: a "Close tabs to the right" or "Close other tabs" question whose tabs were all pinned on another device
  no longer comes back when one of them is unpinned; a pinned tab always shows in front, even in a layout an older
  version saved with it further along; and with more pinned tabs than half the strip holds, they scroll in their own
  group instead of pushing the other tabs out of view.
