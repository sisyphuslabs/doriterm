### Phone

- Coming back to the app after its connection dropped in the background picks the session up within about a second and a half instead of holding your typing for 10 seconds first, and time spent in another app no longer counts against carrying the session on, so it is not given up the moment you return. (#2853)
- A network blip that recovers within a second shows nothing, and "Offline" waits that second too. On a phone terminal, "Reconnecting" is said in the header's line under the title, so the terminal keeps its rows while it reconnects. (#2853)
