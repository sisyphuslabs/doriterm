### Connection and offline wording

- Keys typed while a terminal's connection is down, or while it is still opening, now wait the way keys for a
  reconnecting machine already did: up to 10 seconds after the connection is known to be down and 4 KB, with Ctrl+C
  sent first when it comes back. Beyond that they are dropped, and the terminal says they were not sent, instead of
  arriving minutes later. A paste larger than 4 KB made while the terminal cannot take it is dropped at once with the
  same notice; paste it again once the terminal is back.
