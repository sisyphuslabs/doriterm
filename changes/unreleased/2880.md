### Workspace

- On an iPad in portrait the sidebar drawer slides in from the edge with its backdrop fading in, and slides back out when it closes; Escape closes it again. (#2875)
- Settings fades and scales in over the workspace on a wide screen and fades out when it closes. (#2875)
- A split pane's header and the dimming of the other panes follow focus over a short colour change. (#2875)

### Phone

- Settings slides in from the right like any other screen and slides off when you leave it; Back's sliding screen no longer flashes before it slides. (#2875)
- The keys panel and the input card rise and fall with the keyboard instead of jumping. (#2539)
- With Reduce Motion on, these movements become fades or happen at once. (#2875)
