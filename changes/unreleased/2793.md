### Settings

- Voice input can use Soniox. Its API key is kept in the mesh vault: encrypted on the device that saves it, copied
  between your machines only as ciphertext, and opened on each of your devices with a key held by the OS (Keychain,
  DPAPI) or a browser key that cannot be read out. The key is never shown again, and removing a device moves the vault
  to a new key and asks you to replace the Soniox key. (#2793)
