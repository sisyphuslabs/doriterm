### Connection

- A machine now keeps a dropped session for 10 minutes instead of 2 for the device to come back to, so a phone that spent a few minutes in another app carries its terminals on where they were instead of redrawing them with "Some output may be missing". A machine keeps at most 16 such sessions at once, each holding its terminal process (about 6-8 MB) until it ends, and ends the one that has waited longest first. Under a plan's session limit, a device that opens a new session replaces the one it left waiting instead of being refused. This takes the host from this release; the app needs no update for it.
