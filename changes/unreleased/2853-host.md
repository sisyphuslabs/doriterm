### Connection

- A machine now keeps a dropped session for 10 minutes instead of 2 for the device to come back to, so a phone that spent a few minutes in another app carries its terminals on where they were instead of redrawing them with "Some output may be missing". A machine keeps at most 16 such sessions at once (about 6 MB each) and ends the one that has waited longest first. This takes the host from this release; the app needs no update for it. (#2853)
