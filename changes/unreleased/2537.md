### Workspace

- A device keeps its open tab, the tab each space last showed, its space colours and the agents it has already looked at
  when it reaches the other machines through a different machine (#2537). They used to be kept per machine the app went
  through, so a reconnect through another machine could lose the open tab or change a space's colour. What a device kept
  before is carried over the first time it opens; space colours saved before are taken as the colours of the machine
  the app goes through on that first opening.
