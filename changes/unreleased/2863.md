### Top bar

- The top bar has one order: the tabs with New tab right after the last one, then on the right a "N needs you" pill that opens the next agent waiting for you, the split buttons, and the mic and session menu, divided by hairlines. On a tablet the two split buttons are one Split pane menu. A strip with more tabs than fit fades at its hidden edge and offers All tabs. On a phone, the terminal header shows a compact count of other agents that need you so you can jump straight to them. (#2863)
