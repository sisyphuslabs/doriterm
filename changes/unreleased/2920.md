### Settings

- Settings is laid out like the Herdr apps. On a phone it opens on a list of sections under short captions (Connection,
  Look, Input, More), each a row with a coloured icon that opens the section's page, with Done at the top and one
  search row under the title. On a tablet or a computer the same groups label the side navigation. Rows sit on rounded
  cards on touch screens and stay flat with a mouse, every row has one height, and a value at the end of a row is the
  size of its title. (#2914)
