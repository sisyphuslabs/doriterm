### Machine mesh

- On a phone paired with several machines, a reconnect, or reopening the app after the browser put it away, keeps you
  on the terminal or space you had open, with Reconnecting shown on top, instead of moving you to another machine.
  (#2535)
- A temporary switch to another machine while yours is unreachable is never remembered. (#2535)
- Reopening the app on a terminal waits on that terminal's own screen until it is read, instead of showing the list.
  (#2535)
