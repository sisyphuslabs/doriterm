### Machine mesh

- On a phone paired with several machines, a reconnect keeps you on the terminal or space you had open, with
  Reconnecting shown on top, instead of moving you to another machine. So does a page the browser reloads at that
  terminal or space after putting it away. (#2535)
- A temporary switch to another machine while yours is unreachable is never remembered. (#2535)
- Reopening the app on a terminal waits on that terminal's own screen until it is read, instead of showing the list.
  (#2535)
