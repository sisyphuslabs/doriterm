### Machine mesh

- A browser or phone can be a member of several meshes at once, each knowing it by keys of its own, so no mesh can link it to its identity in another. Joining another mesh adds it instead of refusing (two joins at once, in two tabs, still get two identities), and with more than one mesh Settings lists them with Show to pick the one in view and every machine row names its mesh. Leaving or forgetting one mesh keeps the others, deletes that mesh's keys and its saved push route, and drops it in every open tab; a leave cut short is finished on the next start. A browser's existing membership moves over unchanged. A machine you also pair this browser with directly is reached with the pairing key, so that machine sees both identities. (#2890)
