### Machine mesh

- A browser or phone can be a member of several meshes at once, each knowing it by keys of its own, so no mesh can link it to its identity in another. Joining another mesh adds it instead of refusing, leaving or forgetting one mesh keeps the others, and with more than one mesh Settings lists them with Show to pick the one in view and every machine row names its mesh. A browser's existing membership moves over unchanged. (#2890)
