### Connection and offline wording

- A phone or browser that is still paired no longer asks to pair again after its session ends. When a dropped
  connection moved on to another machine in your mesh that never paired this device, that machine's "unknown device"
  answer ended the reconnect with "Pair it again" and kept the app pointed at it. The app now moves on until it reaches
  the machine it is paired with, and says the device is unknown only when every machine refuses it. (#2759)
