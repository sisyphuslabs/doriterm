### Terminal

- A reconnect puts a reader who was up in history back where they were: the reattach measures their distance from the
  end before the replay empties the buffer, restores it after the reset and after each batch of the replay has painted,
  and tells the terminal (a patched `@wterm/dom`) not to read the emptied buffer as "following output". The hold ends
  once the last batch has painted with nothing left to write, or sooner when the reader scrolls, touches, clicks or
  types; a scroll the terminal makes on its own does not end it. A reader within 5px of the end counts as at it, as the terminal itself counts, so they keep following. A reader
  who was further up than the replayed history reaches (2000 lines) lands at the top of what was replayed. (#1394)
